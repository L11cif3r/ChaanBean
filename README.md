# ChaanBean

## MSME Credit Intelligence, Business Verification & Recovery Platform

ChaanBean is a B2B fintech platform designed to make **business verification, credit assessment, payment recovery, and financial risk management faster and more actionable for the Indian MSME ecosystem**.

The platform is inspired by products such as **LegAn**, but takes a more focused approach. Instead of presenting businesses with a large collection of independent verification services, ChaanBean aims to bring the relevant information together, analyse it, and turn it into a **simple, explainable business decision**.

> **Verify faster. Decide smarter. Recover better.**

---

## What is ChaanBean?

Businesses frequently operate on credit, making it important to understand who they are dealing with before extending credit and what to do when payments are delayed.

ChaanBean is being designed to cover this journey from:

**Business Verification → Risk Assessment → Credit Decision → Payment Monitoring → Recovery → Legal Escalation**

The platform combines information from multiple verification and financial sources and presents it through a unified interface.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Business Verification

ChaanBean brings multiple verification capabilities into one platform, including:

- GST verification and turnover analysis
- Exact GST turnover
- GST filing and compliance reports
- PAN and identity verification
- Mobile-to-PAN and mobile identity checks
- Mobile-to-address verification
- MSME / Udyam verification
- Company information and financial reports
- Director and business information
- Court case history
- FIR checks
- Import / export information
- Address enrichment
- Credit bureau reports

The goal is to reduce the need to manually check multiple platforms and interpret disconnected reports.

---

### Credit Intelligence

ChaanBean is designed to convert the collected information into an understandable risk assessment.

The platform uses a deterministic and explainable risk model to produce:

🟢 **Green** — Lower-risk profile

🟠 **Amber** — Requires caution or additional review

🔴 **Red** — High-risk profile requiring manual review

Risk can be evaluated using factors such as:

- Turnover trends
- GST compliance
- Credit bureau information
- Existing financial obligations
- Litigation indicators
- Identity consistency
- Business standing
- MSME/company information
- Trust network signals

Rather than simply presenting raw reports, ChaanBean aims to show **why a particular risk level was assigned**.

## Payment Recovery

ChaanBean also focuses on the other side of credit:

> **What happens after a business doesn't pay on time?**

The recovery system is designed to automate:

- Payment reminders
- Recovery campaigns
- Delayed-payment tracking
- Escalation workflows
- Outbound voice notifications
- WhatsApp communication
- Email notifications
- SMS communication
- Transaction follow-ups
- Legal notice workflows
